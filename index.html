✺ Maa ki Rasoi

A Day in Maa's Rasoi

This is how every swali is made. No hurry, no shortcuts.

  1. 1. Gundhna

    Maida, rava and ajwain, kneaded firm by hand.

  2. 2. Belna

    Rested, then rolled out thin.

  3. 3. Kaatna

    Cut round and pricked with a fork for the crunch.

  4. 4. Talna

    Slow-fried until golden, in Saffola oil or desi ghee.

Questions, answered

How long do the snacks stay fresh?▾

Kept in an airtight container, they stay crisp for about 3 weeks. We cook in small batches, so you always get fresh snacks.

Where do you deliver?▾

We ship across India. Delivery time and charges for your pincode are confirmed with you on WhatsApp.

How do I pay?▾

Send your order on WhatsApp and we'll confirm it and share the payment details (UPI).

Oil, ghee or air-fried: which should I pick?▾

Ghee is the richest and most aromatic, Saffola oil is perfect for everyday chai, and air-fried is the lighter, healthier choice.

Gift or bulk orders?▾

Yes! Add a gift message in your jhola, or message us on WhatsApp for large orders.
